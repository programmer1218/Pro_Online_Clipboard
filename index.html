What Is Online Clipboard?

Online Clipboard is a free web-based tool that acts as a shared clipboard between any two devices. Instead of relying on a single browser's copy-paste memory, it lets you push text, an image, or a small file to a temporary, encrypted storage slot identified by a random 6-digit code. Open the site on a second device — a phone, laptop, tablet, or even a shared computer — type in that code, and your content appears immediately. There is nothing to install and nothing to configure.

The idea is simple, but it solves a problem almost everyone runs into multiple times a week: getting a piece of information from the device you have it on to the device you actually need it on, right now, without emailing yourself or fighting with a messaging app's compression.

Why Not Just Use Email or WhatsApp?

Emailing yourself a link works, but it means opening a mail client, finding your own address, and waiting for the round trip. Messaging apps like WhatsApp or Telegram are built for conversations, not transfers — they compress images, bury files in chat history, and require both devices to be signed into the same account. Cloud drives like Google Drive or Dropbox require an upload, a sync delay, and often a login on the receiving end too. Online Clipboard skips all of that: there is no account, no chat thread, no folder structure, and no compression. You paste or upload, get a code, and retrieve — usually in under ten seconds end to end.

How Your Data Is Handled

Every transfer is encrypted in transit and stored only long enough to be retrieved — a strict 24-hour window, after which it is permanently and automatically deleted from our servers. We do not ask for an email address, a phone number, or any account details, and we never associate a transfer with a personal identity. You can read the full breakdown on our Privacy Policy page.

Frequently Asked Questions
How does Online Clipboard work? ▾
Your content is temporarily stored on our servers and linked to a unique 6-digit code. Enter that code on any other device to retrieve it instantly. Everything is deleted automatically after 24 hours.
Is Online Clipboard free to use? ▾
Yes, completely free. No signup, no payment, no premium tier. All features are available to everyone at no cost, supported by non-intrusive advertising.
How long does my content stay available? ▾
All content is automatically and permanently deleted after 24 hours. A countdown timer shows exactly when your code expires. There is no way to extend it — just resend if needed.
What file types and sizes are supported? ▾
Any file type up to 5 MB. For images, PNG, JPG, GIF and WebP are supported at full quality. For text, there is no practical character limit.
Is my data private and secure? ▾
All transfers are encrypted in transit. We do not require personal information or log the content of your transfers, and everything is permanently erased after 24 hours.
Do I need to create an account? ▾
No account needed. Just open the site, paste or upload your content, and share the 6-digit code. No registration or email required.
Can multiple people retrieve the same code? ▾
Yes, anyone with the 6-digit code can retrieve the content within 24 hours — great for sharing with a group, a classroom, or a meeting.
What devices and browsers are supported? ▾
All modern browsers — Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge — on phones, tablets, laptops and desktops running iOS, Android, Windows, macOS, or Linux. No plugins required.
What happens if I enter the wrong code? ▾
You will see a "Code not found or expired" message. Double-check the 6 digits and try again. If the code has actually expired, the original sender will need to resend the content.
