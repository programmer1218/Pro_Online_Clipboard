Contact Us

We would love to hear from you — whether it is a bug report, a feature suggestion, a question about how Online Clipboard works, or a business inquiry.

Email

The fastest way to reach us is by email at discretescisyhp1251@gmail.com. We aim to respond to every message within a few business days.

What to Include

To help us respond quickly and accurately, please include as much of the following as applies to your message:

  • A clear description of the issue or question
  • The device and browser you were using (e.g. iPhone Safari, Windows Chrome)
  • Steps to reproduce, if you are reporting a bug
  • Screenshots, if relevant

Bug Reports

If something is not working as expected — a code will not retrieve, an upload fails, or a page looks broken — please let us know. Include the approximate time it happened and, if possible, the 6-digit code involved (codes expire after 24 hours, so please reach out promptly).

Feature Requests

Online Clipboard is actively maintained, and we welcome ideas for new features or improvements. While we cannot guarantee every suggestion will be implemented, we read every message.

Privacy and Business Inquiries

For questions about our Privacy Policy, data handling, or partnership and advertising inquiries, please use the same email address above and mention the topic in your subject line.
