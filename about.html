About Online Clipboard

Online Clipboard is a free, browser-based tool that lets you instantly share text, images, and files between any two devices — without creating an account, installing an app, or going through any complicated setup. All you need is a browser and an internet connection.

Why We Built This

We have all been in this situation: you have something on your phone that you need on your laptop, or vice versa. Maybe it is a link you found while browsing, a photo you just took, a code snippet you are working on, or a PDF someone sent you. The usual options are frustrating. Emailing yourself feels clunky. Messaging apps compress your images. Cloud storage requires syncing and waiting. Bluetooth is unreliable. USB cables are never nearby.

Online Clipboard solves this with the simplest possible approach: paste or upload your content, get a 6-digit code, and enter that code on any other device to retrieve it instantly. No login, no friction, no delay.

Who It Is For

Online Clipboard is built for anyone who regularly works across multiple devices. This includes:

  • Students who switch between a phone and a college computer throughout the day
  • Developers who need to move code snippets, terminal output, or API keys between machines
  • Designers who want to quickly send a screenshot or preview image from one device to another
  • Professionals who work on both a work computer and a personal device and need to move things between them
  • Anyone who wants to share something with a person sitting nearby without going through messaging apps or email

How It Works

When you send content through Online Clipboard, it is temporarily stored on our secure servers and linked to a randomly generated 6-digit code. That code is shown to you immediately after sending. On any other device, you can open Online Clipboard, enter the 6-digit code in the Retrieve tab, and your content appears instantly.

The entire process typically takes under ten seconds from send to receive. No waiting for sync, no file size compression, no account setup.

Every piece of content is automatically and permanently deleted from our servers after 24 hours. We do not retain your data beyond the temporary storage window, and we do not associate it with any personal identity.

What You Can Transfer

Online Clipboard supports three types of content transfers:

  • Text — Any written content, including notes, links, addresses, code, passwords, or plain text up to any reasonable length
  • Images — PNG, JPG, GIF, and WebP image files, transferred at full quality without compression
  • Files — Any file type up to 5 MB, including PDFs, Word documents, spreadsheets, zip archives, and more

Our Commitment to Privacy

Privacy is a core part of how Online Clipboard is designed. We do not require you to create an account or provide any personal information. We do not log the content of what you share. Content is stored only for the duration needed to allow retrieval — a maximum of 24 hours — and then permanently erased.

We use industry-standard encryption for all data in transit. The 6-digit codes are randomly generated and unpredictable. While we recommend against using this tool for highly sensitive material like financial passwords, it is well-suited for everyday personal and professional use.

Completely Free

Online Clipboard is free to use with no limits on the number of transfers, no premium tier required, and no hidden charges. The service is supported by non-intrusive advertising, which allows us to keep it free for everyone. You do not need to pay, register, or sign up to access any feature.

Built for the Modern Web

Online Clipboard works on every modern device and browser — Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge, and others — without any plugins, extensions, or downloads. It is fully responsive and works equally well on phones, tablets, and desktop computers. Whether you are on iOS, Android, Windows, macOS, or Linux, the experience is identical.

The tool also supports QR code generation for each transfer, making it even faster to receive content on a phone by simply scanning the code from a desktop screen.
